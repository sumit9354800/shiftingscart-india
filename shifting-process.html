Process overview

Built to reduce uncertainty at every stage

The steps below show how your move usually goes from first contact to final placement.

01

Enquiry & consultation

The process begins when a customer reaches out by call, WhatsApp, or contact form.

02

Requirement understanding

Move type, locations, timing, and important details are gathered for a clearer quote path.

03

Scheduling

The move is aligned with a suitable date and a better-planned service flow.

04

Packing & preparation

Preparation is handled with attention to what needs safer movement and cleaner coordination.

05

Loading

Items are moved into the vehicle with care and a safe loading order.

06

Transit

The movement stage is managed with a focus on timing and customer confidence.

07

Unloading

At the destination, the unloading stage continues the same careful handling approach.

08

Final placement & completion

The move is brought to a clean close with final placement and completion reassurance.

Process FAQ

Quick answers about how the process works

How does the shifting process begin?

It begins with an request and requirement discussion so the right service path can be planned clearly.

Can I contact ShiftingsCart during the process?

Yes. WhatsApp and call actions remain available across the site so customers can reach support quickly.

Is the process the same for every move type?

The broad flow stays similar, but the exact handling varies based on whether the move is for a home, office, car, or bike.
