Handling standards

Safety, packing, and handling standards

Office shifting works best when categories are separated clearly: documents, workstations, electronics, chairs, storage, and meeting-room assets. Packing, sequence, loading, and destination placement all need to support the business restart goal.

  • Sensitive business items are identified early in the planning stage
  • Electronics and workstation setups are handled with controlled move sequencing
  • Document movement is planned to reduce mix-ups and post-move confusion
  • Movement windows can be aligned around lower-disruption timing where possible
